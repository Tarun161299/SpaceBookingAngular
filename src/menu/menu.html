Our Story

Urban Cafe Restaurant is the culmination of our passion for exceptional flavors and our desire to create a welcoming, modern dining experience.

We offer a curated selection of local wines, beers, handcrafted cocktails, fresh juices, and our own-brand coffee and gelato, delivering quality in every cup and scoop.

For those on the go, we prepare fresh sandwiches and wraps daily, perfect for quick, convenient grab-and-go meals without compromising on freshness or flavour.

We proudly cater for all dietary needs, including gluten-free, coeliac-friendly, dairy-free, nut-free, and vegan options, ensuring everyone feels welcome.

Every day, our kitchen bakes fresh cakes, muffins, and slices, made in-house for the perfect sweet treat.

Urban Vibes Café — fresh food, inclusive choices, and great vibes, every day. ☕🥪🍰
